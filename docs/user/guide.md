# Using Folio

Tap **+** to add a card. Take a straight-on photo or choose one from your library, give the card a name, choose a category, and tap **Add to Folio**. Your photo becomes the card face in the collection. Tap a card to open it; move your finger across the card to see the light and tilt. Use the menu or **Edit card** to change its photo and details, or remove it.

On a native development build with NFC hardware, choose **Link an NFC tag** or **Scan physical card** and hold a compatible NDEF card near the phone. Folio stores the NDEF records on the device. A saved scan is for reference and does not make the phone an emulated copy of every card.
