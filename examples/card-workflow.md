# Example: add an access card

1. Open Folio and tap **+**.
2. Photograph the front of your own card in even light.
3. Name it “Studio North” and choose **Access**.
4. If the card contains a compatible NDEF tag and you are using a development build, tap the NFC link row and scan it.
5. Save. The new card appears first in the collection. Open it to view the photo and scan status.
